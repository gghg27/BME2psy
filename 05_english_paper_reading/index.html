    05 英文论文阅读、翻译与问答¶

    目标：5 分钟抓住 abstract 的逻辑，20 分钟完成翻译和批判，并能口头回答追问。真实论文元数据见 references_papers.md；以下训练材料均为原创考试化摘要。

    1. 五分钟拆 Abstract¶

    按 B-Q-M-R-C-L 在原文旁标记：

    1. Background：已知什么，缺口是什么？
    2. Question：本文到底比较/解释/预测什么？
    3. Methods：谁、什么设计、IV/DV、关键分析？
    4. Results：方向、交互、效应大小/不确定性；不要只圈 p。
    5. Conclusion：作者声称到哪一层：描述、关联、预测、因果、机制？
    6. Limit：样本、操作、测量、统计与推广中最关键的一条。

    口头模板：This study asked whether... The authors used... The main finding was... This supports..., but it does not establish... because...

    2. 翻译纪律¶

    • associated with 译“与……相关”，不译“导致”；predict 在统计语境译“预测”，不自动译“决定”。
    • mediate 先译“统计中介”；只有设计支持时间和因果假设时再谈机制。
    • significant 译“统计显著”，不译“重要”；robust 说明对哪些分析/样本稳健。
    • 先找主干主谓宾，再处理定语从句；方法名保留英文，首次给中文。
    • 数字按“估计值 + CI + p + 方向”成组翻译，不能只留 p 值。

    4. 15 篇 Abstract 模拟训练¶

    每篇都回答同一组问题：A 英译中；B 一句话研究问题；C 设计；D IV/DV；E 解释结果；F 评价设计；G 一个 follow-up。答案见 05_english_paper_reading_answers.md。

    E01 简单：空间注意¶

    Valid spatial cues often facilitate visual processing. Forty adults completed a Posner task in which targets followed valid or invalid cues. Responses were faster after valid cues, whereas accuracy did not differ. The cueing benefit was larger at short than long cue-target intervals. These findings suggest that spatial cues alter the time course of target selection, but they do not determine whether the effect reflects enhanced processing at the cued location or delayed disengagement from invalid locations.

    E02 简单：工作记忆¶

    We tested whether working-memory load changes response variability. Participants completed 1-back and 3-back blocks while reaction time and accuracy were recorded. Higher load reduced accuracy and increased both mean reaction time and trial-to-trial variability. Variability remained higher after controlling for mean speed. Thus, demanding updating may destabilize performance, although the n-back manipulation also changes maintenance and decision difficulty.

    E03 简单：情绪调节¶

    Participants viewed negative pictures and either watched naturally or reappraised their meaning. Reappraisal reduced reported unpleasantness and late positive potential amplitude. The two outcomes were moderately correlated across trials. The results are consistent with reduced sustained emotional processing during reappraisal. Because participants knew the study purpose and no physiological measure was included, reporting strategies remain a plausible explanation.

    E04 简单：疼痛期待¶

    Neutral cues predicted either high or low heat, but identical medium heat was occasionally delivered after both cues. Pain ratings were higher following high-pain cues. Expectancy ratings statistically mediated this difference. The experiment supports a causal effect of cue condition on reported pain under the present procedure; however, the mediation analysis alone does not establish that expectancy is the unique psychological mechanism.

    E05 简单：抑制¶

    Adults performed a stop-signal task before and after one night of restricted sleep. Stop-signal reaction time increased after sleep restriction, while go-trial reaction time also slowed. The authors interpreted this pattern as impaired inhibition. A competing account is a broader reduction in alertness, which should be tested with an independent vigilance measure and a model that separates triggering failures from stopping speed.

    E06 中等：疼痛 EEG¶

    We examined whether nociceptive-evoked gamma oscillations encode pain intensity. Laser stimuli at three energies were delivered to healthy adults. Gamma power increased with both stimulus energy and pain ratings and predicted ratings in held-out trials. Because energy, salience, and pain covaried, the result shows predictive information rather than pain specificity. A matched nonpainful salient condition would strengthen the interpretation.

    E07 中等：焦虑注意偏向¶

    Trait anxiety was related to faster responses when probes replaced threatening faces. The conventional bias score showed low split-half reliability, but a hierarchical trial-level model yielded a more stable anxiety-by-probe-location interaction. This suggests that measurement model choice can change the apparent robustness of attentional bias. Replication across sessions is required before interpreting the effect as an individual trait.

    E08 中等：fMRI 重评¶

    Reappraisal was associated with increased dorsolateral prefrontal activity, reduced amygdala activity, and lower negative affect. Psychophysiological interaction analysis indicated stronger negative coupling between the two regions during reappraisal. These observations are compatible with coordinated regulation, but PPI does not establish a directed inhibitory influence. Effective-connectivity model comparison or perturbation would be needed for a stronger directional claim.

    E09 中等：动态网络¶

    Hidden Markov modelling identified five recurring whole-brain states during rest. Individuals with higher anxiety spent more time in a state characterized by stronger salience-network coupling. State occupancy predicted anxiety in nested cross-validation but failed in an external site. The internal association is promising, whereas the external failure indicates sensitivity to acquisition, population, or preprocessing differences.

    E10 中等：多模态脑龄¶

    Structural MRI and resting-state connectivity were used to estimate brain age. A multimodal model outperformed either modality alone in internal cross-validation. Brain-age gap was larger in a clinical group, but scanner site was correlated with diagnosis. Without site-balanced external validation, the gap may reflect acquisition differences rather than atypical development.

    E11 接近考试：统计交互¶

    A mixed experiment crossed group with memory load. Load impaired accuracy in patients (p=.01) but not controls (p=.18); nevertheless, the group-by-load interaction was not significant and its confidence interval included effects in both directions. The authors claimed a patient-specific deficit. This inference is not justified because a difference in significance is not evidence of a significant difference.

    E12 接近考试：疼痛标志物¶

    A multivariate fMRI signature discriminated painful from nonpainful stimulation and generalized across two laboratories. It also responded to highly salient nonpainful sounds, although less strongly. The pattern is therefore sensitive and partly generalizable but not fully selective for pain. Clinical biomarker claims additionally require individual calibration, incremental validity, and prospective utility.

    E13 接近考试：影像转录组¶

    Regional cortical abnormalities in schizophrenia were spatially correlated with postmortem gene-expression maps enriched for synaptic pathways. The analysis links macroscale anatomy to a plausible molecular context. However, spatial autocorrelation, donor mismatch, and the ecological nature of regional correlations prevent person-level or causal conclusions. Replication with spatial null models and patient-specific molecular data would strengthen the claim.

    E14 接近考试：神经调控¶

    Active and sham stimulation were compared in a randomized study of attention. Active stimulation improved hit rate, but participants correctly guessed their condition above chance because scalp sensations differed. The behavioral effect may reflect neural modulation, expectancy, or differential arousal. Sensation-matched sham, blinding assessment, and an independent arousal measure are necessary before attributing the benefit to the targeted circuit.

    E15 接近考试：跨物种疼痛¶

    Parallel recordings in humans and rodents revealed thalamocortical dynamics that scaled with nociceptive input across species. The cross-species convergence supports a conserved nociceptive process. Yet human pain reports and rodent defensive behavior are not equivalent outcomes, so the study cannot establish a shared subjective pain experience. The strongest conclusion concerns homologous neural responses to potentially harmful stimulation.

    面试高频问题¶

    1. Please distinguish association, prediction, and causal explanation.
    2. Why does external validation matter after cross-validation?
    3. What is the strongest alternative explanation for this abstract?
    4. Which single follow-up experiment would be most informative?
    5. How would you translate “significant” and “account for” in context?
